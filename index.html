Architecture Introduction – Hanyi

Yi Han (he/him), (pronouns: E Han)
WHY YOU GOT INTO ARCHITECTURE?
Because architecture merges system logic with emotional experience—it's where structure meets simulation, and I want to explore its limits using AI.
WHAT DO YOU HOPE TO GET OUT OF THIS COURSE?
To test the boundaries of computational design and architectural expression, and to refine my thesis into a real design framework.
FAVORITE ARCHITECT OR DESIGNER
Kazuyo Sejima, for her subtlety and spatial ambiguity. Also fascinated by Marcos Novak’s ideas on liquid architecture.
WHAT DO YOU WANT TO DO WHEN YOU FINISH DESIGN SCHOOL?
Build a platform combining AI, simulation theory, and real-time design feedback. Possibly launch a startup or continue into research.
HOBBIES OUTSIDE OF ARCHITECTURE
Philosophy, simulation theory, language design, game mechanics, and cognitive modeling.
