System Features

Buildable

Three sets of 1:8 physical prototypes verified geometric adaptability and constructability.

Robustness

The algorithm self-optimizes, avoiding the self-intersections common in traditional offsetcrvonsrf.

Continuity

Brick spacing can be constrained, extreme curvature areas are automatically avoided, and additional bricks can be inserted in extreme regions.

Any Surface

Surfaces determine bricks, rather than bricks determining surfaces.

Any Size and Spacing

Each row of bricks can stably vary in size and spacing.

Any Orientation

Brick alignment can be bound to any defined orientation, no longer limited to horizontal layout.

Typical Input/Output

InputDescription
Target SurfaceSupports complex freeform surfaces

Parameter List [L, W, Joint]Controls brick length, width, and mortar joint

About Porcupine

Porcupine is a Grasshopper plugin for arranging standard units across freeform surfaces, emphasizing constructability, continuity, and curvature conformity.
